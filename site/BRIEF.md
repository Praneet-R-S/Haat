# Haat landing page: brief

Self-authored under explicit creative delegation. The facts, assets and hard rules
come from the team's request; the creative decisions below are authored, not
interviewed. No user quotations are invented.

## 1. Vibe
"A ledger with a maker's hand in it." Editorial print: warm paper, black ink,
natural indigo. References: a well-made exhibition catalogue (plates, captions,
a colophon), an old trade ledger (ruled columns, a red margin rule, entries in a
clerk's hand), indigo-stained hands.

## 2. Journey (authored)
1. Recognition. The craft first: a weaver at the loom, carved blocks, indigo cloth.
2. Tension. Everything between one block printer's cloth and a buyer in Ohio.
   The paperwork piles up on top of the cloth until the cloth is nearly gone.
3. The turn and the peak. "So he sends a voice note." The note plays; the Hindi
   becomes English; the listing builds itself; the price is worked out so his
   rupees survive the fees. Published.
4. After payment. The autopilot writes the ledger: settle, invoice, ship, pay
   the makers, tell the maker. Every PayPal call recorded.
5. "His next buyer might not be a person." A buyer's agent, a spending mandate,
   a human approving in PayPal. MCP for outside agents.
6. Close. A haat is a market. Open the live desk.

## 3. Energy curve
Calm open (slow parallax), rising pressure (the pile), a held breath (one line on
empty paper), the loudest act (indigo flood, sound, assembly), then a steady
working rhythm (ledger lines), a lighter, curious act (chat), and a closing page
that stops moving and holds.

## 4. Feeling curve
| Act | Feeling | What causes it |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Recognition | respect, quiet | real hands and tools, layered and slow, a headline that names the gap |
| 2 Tension | weight, mild dread | paper slips stack and bury the cloth one by one, each a real requirement |
| (silence) | held breath | "So he sends a voice note." alone on paper. Authored silence, not dead scroll |
| 3 Peak | delight, relief | indigo floods the page, the waveform scrubs, Devanagari resolves into English, the listing card assembles beside it, the stamp lands |
| 4 Autopilot | trust | the ledger writes itself, one honest line per PayPal call |
| 5 Agents | curiosity | a buyer's agent works inside a mandate; the human still approves in PayPal |
| 6 Close | resolve | the market at night, two clear doors, nothing fading |

## 5. The peak
"You scroll and his Hindi voice note turns into an English export listing, word
by word, with the price worked out so he still gets what he asked for." Lives in
act 3. It gets the most scroll room (about 6 viewport heights), the only colour
flood on the page, the only audio, and it is preceded by the quietest screen.

## 6. Range from premium-minimal
Editorial catalogue, warmer and denser than premium-minimal: real captions,
mono ledger figures, ruled paper, a red margin rule. No glass, no glow, no gradients
on text.

## 7. One world or distinct scenes
Distinct scenes, bound as one catalogue: each act is a "plate" with its own device.

## 8. Assets
Real stock photographs (Unsplash/Pexels, credited in CREDITS.html). They are not
photographs of the sample maker. The Morocco and Pakistan photographs are not used.
The voice note is the real test-run audio served by the app at /seed/sample-voice-hi.wav.

## Grammar
"Exhibition catalogue." Running head instead of a nav bar (wordmark left, one
door right). Acts are plates with mono captions that credit the photograph. The
close is a colophon page. Lean-on: captions, ruled rules, plate numerals in the
captions only (no section counters). Bans: section counters, scroll cues,
centred-everything, a fading footer.

## Device per act
| Act | Device | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | layered parallax (4 planes: textile plate, arched subject window, carved-block foreground, muslin atmosphere) | depth and craft before any argument |
| 2 | pinned kinetic stack | the pile has to visibly build |
| 3 | scrubbed voice-note signature + colour flood (pinned, longest) | the transformation is the product |
| 4 | horizontal pan across ledger folios, lines writing in | a sequence of steps reads as a row of entries |
| 5 | split layout, chat that reveals as it enters (not pinned) | a conversation, not an animation |
| 6 | resolved close: still plate + colophon, holds | the page stops and offers the doors |

## It's the site where
"It's the site where a block printer's Hindi voice note turns into an export
listing while you scroll, and you can hear it."
